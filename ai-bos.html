AI-BOS

Your business sets the direction.
AI follows

AI-BOS is our framework for designing, building and deploying AI systems around your business needs. It connects your existing systems with AI capabilities, with the controls needed for everyday use and the flexibility to adapt as your requirements evolve.

CONNECTED BUSINESS PROCESSES

AI built around your business processes

We start by understanding the process: where it stalls, which systems it depends on and which decisions need human judgment. That shapes how we connect tools, combine AI with software rules and define review points. Once running, the workflow is monitored and improved as needs change.

Before

Disconnected tools and information

Information is scattered across tools and documents. People spend time searching, switching systems and piecing together what they need.

Coordinate the process across systems

AI-BOS connects existing tools, AI-assisted tasks and business rules into a defined workflow, with permissions, review points and monitoring built in.

What’s built in

Connect existing systems so steps can flow between tools.

Reduce manual handovers by coordinating tasks and approvals.

Keep actions within limits with permissions and guardrails.

Bring people in for decisions and exceptions that need review.

Monitor quality over time and flag failures for review.

Adapt as needs change by updating models, integrations and rules.

After

Less searching, fewer handovers

People work from one connected view instead of gathering context by hand, and tasks move without waiting on someone to assemble the inputs.

A PROCESS IN MOTION

A clear path through each task

AI-BOS coordinates the steps, checks and decisions that carry a task through your systems. Human review, permitted actions and responsibilities are defined around your process, so routine tasks can progress and exceptions reach the right person.

  1. People & systems

    Receive

    A request, a system event or a scheduled check starts the process with the available context.

  2. Software rules

    Check

    Software checks the input, applies agreed rules and gathers the records the process is permitted to use.

  3. AI assistance

    Prepare

    AI helps interpret the context and prepare a recommendation, response or proposed action.

  4. Human judgment

    Review

    Where review is required, an authorised person checks the evidence, makes changes and decides how to proceed.

  5. Connected systems

    Act & record

    Connected systems carry out the permitted action, such as updating a record or sending a response, and record what happened.

What happens when something needs attention?

Information is missing

The system requests clarification from the person responsible before continuing with the affected step.

A decision needs review

The system sends the case and its context to an authorised reviewer, who decides how to proceed.

An action fails

The system records the issue and notifies the responsible owner, who investigates and resolves it.

CONTROL AND VISIBILITY

Keep control of how AI operates
