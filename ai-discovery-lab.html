AI DISCOVERY LAB

Could AI help with your business challenge?

Understand whether AI can help your business before committing to a build. Together, we identify where it can make a useful difference and recommend a next step grounded in your work, information and systems.

A USEFUL STARTING POINT

Where the conversation can begin

A process needs improving

Repeated effort, delays or inconsistent results are getting in the way. You want to understand whether AI could help and where simpler changes would be enough.

An AI opportunity needs assessing

You have an idea, or several possibilities, but need to understand which is worth pursuing and what it would require.

A pilot needs a way forward

You’ve tested an idea and want to understand what it would take to use it reliably in everyday operations.

HOW WE APPROACH IT

Understand the process before deciding how to proceed

We assess the opportunity against the improvement you need, the constraints of your systems and the effort required to make it work.

  1. 01

    Map the current process

    We trace a real task with the people doing it. Where does time go, what causes rework and which improvement would matter most?

    OutputA process map and starting measures, or a plan to establish them.

  2. 02

    Check what’s in place

    We inspect representative information and the systems holding it. Is the information usable, can it be accessed and where must a person decide?

    OutputThe data, integration and review requirements, with gaps and owners.

  3. 03

    Compare the approaches

    We compare AI with software rules and process changes. Does the benefit hold up once corrections, human review and operating costs are included?

    OutputA comparison of options and a focused test plan where needed.

  4. 04

    Recommend a way forward

    We weigh the findings against the business objective. We identify what is worth investing in, what remains uncertain and what should happen next.

    OutputA recommendation with its reasoning, scope and next decision.

THE OUTCOME

What the assessment helps you decide

These examples show how discovery can turn a business question into a practical decision: what to improve, where to start or how to take a pilot forward.

When a process needs improving

Spend less time preparing assessments

Could AI organise financial documents so advisers have more time to assess the business? We examine extraction, rule-based checks and the corrections people still need to make.

The decisionWhich preparation tasks are worth automating, and what test would establish whether they reduce total effort.

When an AI opportunity needs assessing

Choose a useful first AI application

Where would easier access to internal knowledge make the biggest difference? We compare recurring questions, time spent finding answers and whether approved sources can support reliable responses.

The decisionWhich task and team to start with, which information to connect and how to evaluate the benefit.

When a pilot needs a way forward

Establish a path beyond the pilot

Can an AI support assistant help with real customer requests? We examine how it uses customer records, handles missing information and passes cases to staff, including the effort needed to correct its responses.

The decisionWhether to take the assistant into everyday use, test it further or change direction, based on response quality, staff effort and integration needs.

WORKING TOGETHER

How your team and ours work together

To reach decisions like these, we combine your team’s knowledge of the work with our business and technical assessment. We agree who needs to contribute and what to examine when we scope the engagement.

Who to involve

Bring the people who know the day-to-day process, the business owner who will decide what happens next, and colleagues who can explain the relevant systems and data. Together, we agree the problem to investigate and what improvement would be valuable.

What to bring

Bring examples of the work you want to improve: documents, requests or tasks, including difficult cases. Share any available measures of time, effort or errors, and known system or access limits. We identify missing information together.

What we take care of

We map the process, assess the information and systems, and compare AI, conventional automation and process changes against the business objective. We weigh potential benefit, implementation effort and risks, then prepare a recommendation explaining what to pursue, why and what the next step requires.

BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few practical questions

How long does it take, and what does it cost?

We agree the scope, timeline, deliverables and fee with you before starting. These are based on the business question, the information available and whether a technical test is needed.

How much time will our team need to contribute?

We agree who needs to contribute and how much time to set aside before starting. Your team helps demonstrate the work, review examples, clarify system constraints and discuss the findings.

What if we cannot share data or access systems yet?

We agree what can be reviewed and which questions can be answered with it. Where access limits a recommendation, we identify what is needed before making the next decision.

Will we receive a working prototype?

You receive a recommendation and the reasoning behind it. We include a prototype or technical test when it is needed to answer the business question and agreed in the scope.

What happens after the assessment?

We review the findings and next step with you. You decide whether to proceed, test further, address a prerequisite, choose another approach or stop. Any follow-on work is scoped separately.
