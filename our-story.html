HOW IT STARTED

It began with a team worth staying for

Every company has a first chapter, and ours began in Romania, where Simon Obstbaum, our founder, was working with a group of engineers who were quietly, remarkably good at what they did. He had come for a project. He stayed for the people, and along the way he fell for the country too.

When the project ended, he built a company around the people he did not want to leave behind and called it Yopeso, short for YOur PErsonal SOftware. The name still means what it meant then. We build what you need, and we stand behind it as if it were our own.

OUR VALUES

What drives us

If there is one habit that holds all of this together, it is that we care. For our clients and for our people, because you cannot do one well without the other. Three values describe how that shows up in the work.

Authenticity

We say how things are, even when it would be easier not to. It is uncomfortable for a moment and better for everyone afterward, and it gets far better outcomes than polite silence ever did.

Curiosity

Technology changes faster every year, and the people who thrive here are the ones who find that exciting rather than exhausting. We look for people who find that exciting, and who keep learning long after anyone asked them to.

Ambition

We are still growing, and we are not done. A company that stops pushing itself falls behind, so we keep raising the bar for the work we put our name to.

THE EXPERIENCE THAT SHAPED US

Delivering is only half the job

In our first decade, we were a delivery company, and a good one. We built what was specified, on time and to standard. Then we noticed our most successful projects were the ones where we challenged the brief. Our clients did not need another pair of hands. They needed a partner with an opinion.

Today, consultation comes first. We say plainly what we think, with the long-term outcome in mind, even when that means recommending less. We bring the same approach to AI: we start with the business, not the technology. The AI Discovery Lab finds where AI makes sense, and AI-BOS builds it into the way your business already runs.

WHAT SETS US APART

No layers. No bragging.
Someone picks up.

We are a boutique by design, and we have stayed one as the company grew past 300 people. Our leadership team is easy to reach for every client. When something goes wrong, as it eventually does in twenty years of building software, a senior person takes ownership and helps resolve it. There are no layers between you and the people who can make a decision.

That is part of why people stay. Our client relationships typically last five years or more. Many of our colleagues stay for four to eight years, and some for longer. That means the engineers who built your first release are often still around for the next ones. It is no accident. It comes from honesty and transparency, with our clients and with each other.
